Keep your PiTunes smart playlists

Open this page in the same browser and at the same PiTunes address that holds your playlists. Download a copy before replacing or erasing the old installation.

The export contains smart playlist definitions and PiTunes viewing preferences. It leaves your browser data unchanged.

In PiTunes Native, open Settings → Library → Import browser data. Native rule playback is still being migrated; imported definitions are preserved.
